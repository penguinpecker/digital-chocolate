PenguCrush

Beyond the Horizon

Android · In development

Ten islands. A hundred adventures.

Pack light. Crush big. Join Kip, Rook and Juno on a match-3 expedition from Frosthaven to the Aurora Crown. Rescue stranded chicks, collect colourful treasures and bring the islands’ beacons back to life.

100playable levels
10islands to discover
9different board shapes

A new twist around every island.

Break through layered ice and stubborn coral. Cut anchored nets, light lanterns and navigate currents that shift the board. Nine board shapes and multi-stage beacon finales keep you thinking as the expedition unfolds.

Odd birds. Good company.

Kip, Rook and Juno each bring a different companion power. Pick your partner, charge their ability and make the most of rockets, puffers and pearls. Complete daily quests, earn star chests and collect a relic from every island.

Bring the adventure along.

The current Android build plays offline, saves progress on your device and offers free retries. There are no advertisements or cash purchases in this build. Store availability will be announced when the game is ready.
