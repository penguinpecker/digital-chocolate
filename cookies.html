Cookies Policy

LAST UPDATED · 12 MAY 2026

This Cookies Policy explains how Digital Chocolate uses cookies and similar technologies on our websites and inside our applications. Read alongside our Privacy Policy.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser. We also use related technologies — local storage, session storage, and pixels — which behave similarly. We refer to all of these as "cookies" below.

2. The cookies we use

Strictly necessary

Required for the Services to function — for example, remembering your consent choices, maintaining a session, or keeping you signed in. These cannot be disabled without breaking the Services.

Functional

Remember your preferences — language, audio settings, last-played title, or which wallet you most recently connected. These improve the experience but aren't essential.

Analytics

Aggregate, privacy-respecting analytics that help us understand which pages people visit and which features get used. We do not use cookies to build personal advertising profiles.

3. Third-party cookies

Some functionality (embedded videos, payment processors, wallet connectors, analytics vendors) sets cookies under its own domain. Their cookie policies govern those cookies. We don't control them.

4. Managing cookies

You can:

  • Reject non-essential cookies via our consent banner where shown;
  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings;
  • Use private/incognito browsing to limit persistent cookies;
  • Opt out of analytics via your device or browser controls (e.g., DNT).
Blocking strictly-necessary cookies will break sign-in, gameplay state, and on-chain interactions. Disable them only if you are willing to lose those features.

5. Changes

We may update this Cookies Policy as the technologies we use evolve. Material changes will be reflected here with a new "Last Updated" date.

6. Contact

Questions? Reach us via the contact page.
