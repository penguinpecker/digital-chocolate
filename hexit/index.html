Coming to Solana Seeker

A price chart you can play.

A line moves through a honeycomb of possibilities. Each hex represents a price range at a future moment. Choose where you think the line will travel, make your prediction and follow it live. Timing and price matter together.

Read. Pick. Watch.

  1. Read the board.Watch the live price line. Look ahead to the open hexes and compare their time windows, price ranges and displayed multipliers.
  2. Pick your hex.Choose your amount and double-tap a tile to place a prediction. The current web game uses test tokens.
  3. Watch the line.A prediction wins when the price line enters the chosen hex during its time window. If it misses, the amount placed on that hex is lost.

Every move in view.

Open predictions, live results and recent activity keep the action visible. Daily, weekly and all-time leaderboards give you different ways to follow your progress. The touch layout brings the same board to a smaller screen.

Next stop: Solana Seeker.

The current web game is available to explore with test tokens. A Solana Seeker edition is planned; dApp Store availability will be announced when it is ready.
