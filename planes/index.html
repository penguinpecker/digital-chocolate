Planes

Dodge. Collect. Climb.

Available on Solana Seeker

The sky keeps getting busier.

Planes is an arcade survival game built around a simple challenge: keep flying. Steer through pursuing missiles, collect star coins and hold your nerve as the waves get faster. A sharp turn can save a run. An extra star can make it your best.

Take off. Stay sharp.

  1. Outmanoeuvre the missiles.Use tight turns and open space to escape homing missiles. The longer you survive, the more demanding the sky becomes.
  2. Sweep up the stars.Collect star coins while you fly. Your survival time and collected stars contribute to your score.
  3. Build your fleet.Spend earned coins to unlock planes with different turning, coin-collection and defensive perks.

Find your favourite way to fly.

Choose from eight planes, from your starter aircraft to the B52. Upgraded planes bring sharper handling, coin magnets and collection bonuses; the B52 adds a shield that can absorb one hit. Try the fleet and find the plane that suits your turns.

Your progress travels with you.

Connect a Solana wallet to sync your coins and owned planes, restore progress and submit runs to the leaderboard. Leaderboard submissions and optional coin-pack purchases use SOL, with transactions reviewed in your wallet.

Play on Solana Seeker.

Open this page on your Seeker and use the button below to visit the Planes listing in the Solana dApp Store. On a desktop, continue browsing here and open the same page on your phone when you are ready to play.
